# SchoolSync — AI Video Maker Script
### Format: Scene-by-scene prompt for AI video tools (InVideo AI, Pictory, HeyGen, Synthesia, etc.)
### Total Duration: ~6 minutes | Style: Professional SaaS product demo

---

## GLOBAL STYLE INSTRUCTIONS
Give these to the AI video tool before anything else:

> **Visual style:** Clean, modern, professional. Blue and white color palette. Bright lighting. Corporate/tech feel.
> **Tone:** Confident, helpful, trustworthy. Like a product demo from a Silicon Valley startup but tailored for West Africa.
> **Music:** Upbeat but professional background instrumental. Subtle throughout. Slightly louder at opening and closing.
> **Text overlays:** Bold white text with slight drop shadow. Blue highlight color: #2d4fd6.
> **Transitions:** Smooth fade or slide between scenes. No flashy effects.
> **Audience:** School principals, proprietors, and administrators in Liberia, West Africa.

---

## SCENE 1 — OPENING HOOK
**Duration:** 0:00 – 0:30

**VISUAL:**
Show a busy African school — students walking through gates, teachers in classrooms, administrators at desks with stacks of paper registers. Quick cuts. Busy, slightly chaotic feeling.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "If you are running a school in Liberia — and you are still using paper registers, spreadsheets, or WhatsApp to manage your students — this video is for you."

**TEXT ON SCREEN:**
> Are you still managing your school on paper?

**TRANSITION:** Fade to black, then fade in SchoolSync logo on clean white background.

## SCENE 3 — THE PROBLEM
**Duration:** 1:00 – 1:30

**VISUAL:**
Split screen or quick cuts showing the problems:
- A teacher searching through a pile of paper registers
- A parent on the phone asking about their child's grades
- A bursar with a messy handwritten ledger
- A stack of unfinished report cards on a desk

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Every term, Liberian school administrators face the same struggles. Attendance registers get lost. Fee records do not match. Parents call asking about their child's grades and you have to search through papers. Report cards take days to prepare. SchoolSync solves all of this."

**TEXT ON SCREEN (bullet points appearing one by one):**
> ✗ Lost attendance registers
> ✗ Missing fee records
> ✗ Slow report card generation
> ✗ No parent visibility

**TRANSITION:** Wipe to clean dashboard screen.
